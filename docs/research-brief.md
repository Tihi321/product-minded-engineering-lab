# RB2 research brief (checked 21 September 2026)

This brief separates published facts and company claims from my inferences. Links point directly to official RB2 pages. Compensation and vacancy details can change; confirm them with the recruiter before relying on them.

## Business, products, and cases — published facts and claims

RB2 says it has built software for wholesalers and other B2B organisations since 2005, combining product data, customer agreements, integrations, exceptions, and order flows. It describes an approach that starts with judgement, connects to real ERP/PIM/WMS/inbox systems, and proves one workflow against one KPI before scaling. [Company overview](https://www.rb2.nl/en/about)

The public product portfolio includes AI agents such as OrderPilot, MatchPilot, and QuotePilot, alongside commerce/platform work and partnerships shown on the company page. OrderPilot says it reads email, Word, Excel, CSV, scans, and other files; identifies customers; matches lines using product numbers/EANs, descriptions, and prior confirmed choices; routes uncertainty to people; and sends checked orders to an ERP. Its INSPIRED case claims 9× faster processing, 60–80% lower processing costs, and six weeks to a working solution. Those performance figures are RB2's published case claims, not independent verification. [OrderPilot](https://www.rb2.nl/en/ai-agents/orderpilot) · [Company overview](https://www.rb2.nl/en/about)

RB2's public case/insight material references complex commerce platforms and integrations. Its technical-foundation article names domain events for products, orders, and customer information; architecture and integration workshops; infrastructure and data migration; and a proof-of-concept approach. It explicitly mentions headless commerce examples such as BigCommerce and commercetools and links platform work around CoreConnect, Contentful, and commercetools. [Technical foundation](https://www.rb2.nl/en/insights/the-technical-foundation-for-a-successful-start-of-a-development-project)

## Lead Engineer vacancy — published facts

The current English vacancy describes a hybrid Purmerend Lead Engineer who leads engineering behind long-lived scalable commerce platforms and short innovation sprints. Listed responsibilities are leading a small international team across Purmerend, Lisbon, and Xiamen; staying hands-on; turning ambiguous business goals into technical plans; proposing lower-effort alternatives; shipping validated prototypes; owning long-term architecture; and driving AI-assisted development standards. It asks for Node.js/TypeScript, cloud/serverless experience, team leadership, product thinking, and comfort with tools such as Copilot, Cursor, and LLM integration. [Product-minded Lead Engineer vacancy](https://www.rb2.nl/en/careers/product-minded-lead-engineer)

The vacancy states that teams run on approximately 75% AI-assisted development and frames the job as helping define how an engineering team designs, ships, and operates with AI as a core workflow. Treat “75%” as the employer's stated approximate claim, not a measured fact independently audited by this project.

The current vacancy lists €6,000–€6,750 gross per month based on 40 hours, bonus up to two months' salary plus a 5% additional contribution, 24/26 vacation days, an extra day off per six months without sick leave, hybrid working from the Purmerend area, training/tooling/time for AI, laptop choice, phone allowance, daily lunch, travel allowance, bike plan, and team events. It says to skip a generic cover letter and instead send a product idea or technical trade-off with the CV to `jobs@rb2.nl`. [Lead Engineer vacancy](https://www.rb2.nl/en/careers/product-minded-lead-engineer)

The related public Backend Tech Lead Node.js page adds a useful second-role signal: roughly half time in Node.js/TypeScript, scalable architecture, AWS/Azure cloud, international leadership, Dutch and English, and ownership from early validation through metrics and learnings. Its listed salary/benefits differ from the current English Lead Engineer page, so do not merge the offers; clarify which vacancy and terms apply. [Backend Tech Lead Node.js](https://www.rb2.nl/en/careers/backend-tech-lead-node-js)

## Hiring process and culture — published facts

The careers page describes first response, meeting the team, a deeper technical session for technical roles, and an offer discussion. It describes small teams, ownership, practical delivery, and one team across Purmerend, Lisbon, and Xiamen. [Careers and process](https://www.rb2.nl/en/jobs?from=careers) · [Careers](https://www.rb2.nl/en/careers)

The company page lists Purmerend as head office and the three locations as one integrated team. The careers pages describe work close to client processes, AI-first without hype, small teams, and real operational outcomes. These are official culture statements, not guarantees about every team or project. [About rb2](https://www.rb2.nl/en/about) · [Careers](https://www.rb2.nl/en/careers)

## Inference for this learning project

The lab mirrors the public problem shape—messy order input, product data, deterministic identifiers, confidence, human exceptions, metrics, and an ERP boundary—without copying proprietary interfaces or claiming to represent RB2 internals. The Lead Engineer material suggests demonstrating hands-on TypeScript plus leadership through explicit boundaries, safe failure, measurable outcomes, and a small vertical slice. That is an inference from public material, not an RB2 hiring rubric.
